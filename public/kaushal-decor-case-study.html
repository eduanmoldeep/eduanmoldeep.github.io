Unified Management Platform for Three Furnishing Stores

One admin panel, one mobile app, three stores — replacing spreadsheets and handwritten bills with real-time sales, PDF generation, and offline-capable mobile access.

The Challenge

Lakshya Sangtani manages three furnishing stores under the same roof — Kaushal Decor, Pratap Home Furnishings, and Lakshya Furnishers. Each store had its own staff, product lines, and customer base, but all three were running on a patchwork of spreadsheets, handwritten bills, and informal delivery notes.

The specific pain points that drove the project:

  • No unified billing — every sale had to be manually written up and tracked across three separate ledgers
  • No mobile access — staff could only log sales or check stock when they were physically at a desktop computer
  • No delivery trail — delivery confirmations were verbal or informal, making disputes difficult to resolve
  • Zero cross-store visibility — there was no single view of how the business was performing across all three locations

What We Built

Web Admin Panel — Laravel 11 + Filament 3.2

A full-featured admin panel that manages all three stores from one dashboard. Sales and billing with fiscal-year bill numbering and one-click PDF generation. Product catalog with categories and master products shared across stores. Customer management with full purchase history. Delivery memos with PDF export. Role-based access control so each store's staff only sees what they need.

REST API — Express.js (Node.js)

A purpose-built backend that powers the mobile app. Full CRUD for all entities — sales, customers, products, payments, delivery memos. JWT authentication with refresh tokens. Rate limiting, audit logs, and 96 automated tests covering every endpoint. Built to OWASP Top 10 security standards.

Mobile App — Flutter (web + mobile)

A cross-platform app that lets field staff do the full sales workflow from a phone: create a sale, add line items, record payments, manage customers, and generate PDF bills on the spot. Biometric login (fingerprint / face ID) for quick, secure access. Offline-first architecture — sales recorded without connectivity sync automatically when the device reconnects.
